Why Atlanta Employers Choose Fortis

Atlanta is our home market. We know the neighborhoods, the industrial corridors, the commute patterns, and the workforce. When you need workers in College Park, Norcross, Union City, or the Perimeter, we know exactly who is available and how fast we can get them to you.

Atlanta's economy has never been more competitive for hourly workers. The city now anchors major operations in logistics, film production, healthcare, and finance, all competing for the same pool of reliable hourly workers. The agencies that win are the ones with deep, pre-screened worker pools. That is what we built.
