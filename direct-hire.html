Our Process

Six steps from opening to offer

We move with urgency but we don't cut corners. Here's exactly what to expect when you work with our direct hire team.

Intake Meeting

We start with a real conversation. We learn the role, the team, and what a successful hire looks like 6 months from now.

Active Search

We tap our Atlanta network, post strategically, and directly recruit passive candidates who aren't applying elsewhere.

Deep Screening

We interview every candidate thoroughly: skills, experience, culture fit, salary alignment, and genuine motivation.

Curated Shortlist

You receive a shortlist of 3 to 5 candidates with detailed write-ups. No spam, no filler. Every person is a real contender.

Interview Coordination

We schedule, prep both sides, and follow up after every interview to keep momentum and gather honest feedback.

Offer & Onboarding

We help you craft a competitive offer and stay involved through acceptance. We also check in at 30, 60, and 90 days.
