The Process

From request to ready in as little as 24 hours

We've refined this process over hundreds of placements so it's as painless as possible for you.

1

Submit your request

Tell us what you need: role, location, start date, shift duration, and number of workers. Takes about 3 minutes.

2

We review and match

Your account manager reviews your request and pulls candidates from our active worker pool. We prioritize workers who've performed well in similar roles.

3

You get a confirmation

We confirm your workers and send over their names, backgrounds, and any relevant certifications before they arrive.

4

Workers arrive ready

We handle onboarding briefings so your workers understand the expectations on day one. No hand-holding required from your team.

After every placement we check in. If something isn't working, we make it right immediately. Your account manager is always reachable.

Our Standards

We only send people we would hire ourselves

Every worker in the Fortis pool has gone through our screening process. We don't cut corners here because a bad placement costs you more than it costs us.

  • Identity verification and I-9 authorization
  • Criminal background check
  • Employment history verification
  • In-person or video interview
  • Skills assessment for technical roles
  • Reference checks from previous employers
